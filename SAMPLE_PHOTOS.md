# Sample photos — prompts, if you want photographs instead of placeholders

The sample ships with schematic placeholders. These prompts produce **generic** industrial
equipment, not any real plant. Keep it that way: the point of a sample is that it belongs to nobody.

Use one prompt per equipment `kind` in `seed.js`. Save as `photos/<assetid>_<slot>.jpg`
(for example `photos/A-010_1.jpg`). Four-by-three, no people, no logos, no legible plant names.

| kind | prompt |
|---|---|
| compressor | Reciprocating gas compressor on a concrete skid inside a steel-framed compressor building, documentary photo, overhead lighting, no people, no signage |
| motor | Large low-voltage electric motor coupled to a pump on a skid, industrial plant, documentary photo, no people, no logos |
| vessel | Vertical pressure vessel with a level gauge and nozzles, outdoors in a gas plant, overcast light, documentary photo, no people |
| pump | Small positive-displacement pump with a stainless suction line and a pressure gauge, industrial skid, documentary photo, no people |
| exchanger | Air-cooled heat exchanger bank with fan guards, outdoors, gas plant, documentary photo, no people |
| xmtr | Pressure transmitter with a stainless impulse line and a manifold on a pipe stand, close-up, documentary photo, no legible tag text |
| psv | Spring-loaded pressure safety valve on a vessel nozzle with a test tag, close-up, documentary photo, no legible text |
| valve | Actuated ball valve with a positioner on a process line, close-up, documentary photo, no legible text |
| meter | Orifice meter run with flanges and a flow computer enclosure, outdoors, documentary photo, no people |
| analyzer | Gas chromatograph in a stainless enclosure with sample lines, analyzer shelter, documentary photo, no legible text |
| panel | Motor control center with closed doors in an electrical room, documentary photo, no people, no legible labels |

Slot guide for a believable set per asset: 1 close-up of the component, 2 the tag plate (blank or
illegible is fine), 3 the nameplate (blank is fine), 4 the surrounding piping, 5 the whole unit from
ten paces back.
